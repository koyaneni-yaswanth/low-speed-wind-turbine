## 2. Foundational Wind Energy & Aerodynamic Literature

1. **Betz, A.** (1920). *"Das Maximum der theoretisch möglichen Ausnützung des Windes durch Windmotoren."* *Zeitschrift für das gesamte Turbinenwesen*, 26, 307–309. (Derivation of the theoretical maximum power coefficient $C_{p,\max} = \frac{16}{27} \approx 0.593$).
2. **Selig, M. S., & Guglielmo, J. J.** (1997). *"High-Lift Low Reynolds Number Airfoil Design."* *Journal of Aircraft*, 34(1), 72–79. DOI: 10.2514/2.2137. (Aerodynamic rationale and polar data for Selig S1223 high-camber profile).
3. **Wu, B., Lang, Y., Zargari, N., & Kouro, S.** (2011). *Power Conversion and Control of Wind Energy Systems.* IEEE Press / John Wiley & Sons. ISBN: 978-0-470-59365-3. (Reference textbook preserved in repository archives: wind generator topologies, DFIG converter sizing, and pitch/torque control dynamics).
4. **Manwell, J. F., McGowan, J. G., & Rogers, A. L.** (2010). *Wind Energy Explained: Theory, Design and Application.* (2nd ed.). John Wiley & Sons.
5. **Burton, T., Jenkins, N., Sharpe, D., & Bossanyi, E.** (2011). *Wind Energy Handbook.* (2nd ed.). John Wiley & Sons.

## 4. International & National Standards

1. **IEC 61400-2**: *Wind energy generation systems – Part 2: Small wind turbines.* International Electrotechnical Commission. (Specifies structural safety, aerodynamic design requirements, mechanical load cases, and test procedures for small turbines with swept area $\le 200\text{ m}^2$).
2. **IEC 61400-12-1**: *Wind turbines – Part 12-1: Power performance measurements of electricity producing wind turbines.* International Electrotechnical Commission. (Methodology for anemometer placement, binning, and power curve calibration).
3. **IS 12383 / Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)**: *Code of practice for design and installation of wind pumping and power systems.*
