### 1. Pawan Shakti Technical & Business Briefing Note

### 2. VNIT Department of Electrical Engineering Technical Presentation
- **File Reference**: `Presentation.pdf` / `Presentation.pptx` (Archived in project root)
- **Presenter / Mentor**: Dr. Jawahar Bhukya, Assistant Professor, EEE Department, VNIT Nagpur
- **Key Contents**:
  - Detailed modal and time-domain analysis of Wind Energy Conversion Systems (WECS).
  - Small signal stability analysis, sub-synchronous oscillations (SSO), and DFIG/converter dynamics under grid fault conditions.

### 3. Betz Limit & Classical Momentum Theory Reference
- **File Reference**: `Betz limit.pptx` (Archived in project root)
- **Key Contents**:
  - First-principles mathematical derivation of the Betz limit ($C_{p,\max} = 16/27 = 0.593$) based on 1D axial momentum theory and conservation of mass/energy.
  - Energy path from atmospheric flow to grid interface.
